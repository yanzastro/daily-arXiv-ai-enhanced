*Christian Poelitz,Finale Doshi-Velez,Siân Lindley*

Main category: cs.HC

TL;DR: 论文提出了一个基于人类协作理论的新基准，用于评估人机协作中的共同基础建立，通过协作解谜任务验证了基准的有效性，并揭示了人机交互与人人协作的差异。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 随着AI在日常生活中的深入应用，需要从简单的信息助手转变为真正的协作伙伴。有效协作依赖于共同基础（共享信念、假设、目标和情境意识），但这一概念在人机协作研究中关注有限。论文旨在填补这一空白，建立基于人类协作理论的评估框架。

Method: 基于人类协作理论和实证研究设计新基准，采用协作解谜任务，要求迭代交互、联合行动、指称协调和误解修复。通过验证性用户研究，让人类参与者与AI协作完成任务，评估基准的有效性。

Result: 基准成功复现了人类协作中的理论和实证发现，同时揭示了人机交互与人人协作的明显差异。验证研究表明该基准能够有效评估人机协作中的共同基础建立过程。

Conclusion: 论文提出的基准为研究人机协作中的共同基础提供了有效工具，既验证了人类协作理论在AI环境中的适用性，也揭示了人机交互的特殊性，为人机协作系统设计提供了重要参考。

While common ground is a central concept in human collaboration, it has received limited attention in studies of human-AI collaboration. In this paper, we introduce a new benchmark grounded in theories and empirical studies of human-human collaboration. The benchmark is based on a collaborative puzzle task that requires iterative interaction, joint action, referential coordination, and repair under varying conditions of situation awareness. We validate the benchmark through a confirmatory user study in which human participants collaborate with an AI to solve the task. The results show that the benchmark reproduces established theoretical and empirical findings from human-human collaboration, while also revealing clear divergences in human-AI interaction.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统固定天线系统在多组多播通信中存在性能限制，需要设计更灵活的天线配置方案来提升系统公平性。夹持天线系统（PASS）通过可重构的PA布局和资源分配优化，旨在改善多组多播通信中的最大最小公平性。

Method: 提出了三种多址接入方案：1) TIN：推导了最优功率分配的闭式解，并开发了顺序逐元素优化（SEO）方法进行PA布局；2) NOMA：开发了结合二分搜索的递归功率分配框架，并采用分层目标评估（HOE）机制简化PA位置更新的SEO过程；3) TDMA：包含PS和PM两种协议，PS协议下PA位置单独优化后时间-功率分配作为凸问题求解，PM协议下通过KKT条件联合优化PA位置和时间-功率资源。

Conclusion: 夹持天线系统通过灵活的天线重配置和资源分配优化，能够显著提升多组多播通信的公平性。TDMA-PS方案在大多数情况下表现最佳，而NOMA在高功率和异构场景下具有竞争力。该研究为下一代无线通信系统的多址接入设计提供了重要参考。

Abstract: This paper aims to design multiple access (MA) schemes to improve the max-min fairness (MMF) for pinching antennas (PAs)-based multigroup multicast communications, where PA placement and resource allocation are jointly optimized. Numerical results demonstrate that: i) the pinching-antenna system (PASS) architecture significantly outperforms traditional fixed-antenna systems. ii) TDMA-PS achieves superior performance by fully leveraging the flexible PA reconfiguration and benefiting from interference-free transmission, whereas TIN serves as a practical lower-bound solution due to its simplicity despite its limited performance. iii) NOMA consistently outperforms TDMA-PM and, in high transmit power regimes with heterogeneous multicast group distributions, can even surpass the performance achieved by TDMA-PS.

### [5] [Score-Based Conditional Flow Models for MIMO Receiver Design with Superimposed Pilots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21654)

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: MIMO系统中精确的信道状态信息至关重要，但叠加导频(SIP)在降低开销的同时引入了严重的导频污染和数据干扰，使得联合信道估计与数据检测变得复杂。传统方法需要大量标注数据且适应性有限。

Abstract: Accurate channel state information (CSI) is vital for multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) systems. However, superimposed pilots (SIP), which reduce overhead, introduce severe pilot contamination and data interference, complicating joint channel estimation and data detection. Furthermore, it integrates receiver-side priors to ensure stable, data-consistent inference. Extensive simulation results across various MIMO configurations demonstrate that CFM-Rx consistently outperforms conventional estimators and state-of-the-art data-driven receivers, achieving notable gains in channel estimation accuracy and symbol detection robustness, particularly under severe pilot contamination.
