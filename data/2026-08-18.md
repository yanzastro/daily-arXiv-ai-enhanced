### [1] [$\texttt{Flip-Team}$: Cooperative Takeover Games with Stochastic Human Override](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16311)
*Sandeep Banik,Naira Hovakimyan*

Main category: cs.HC

TL;DR: 本文提出了一个基于合作博弈论的共享自主（shared autonomy）权威切换框架。通过将控制切换问题形式化为具有相同利益的动态博弈，将权威转移嵌入系统动力学中，获得最优切换策略而非启发式规则。在随机人类覆盖（stochastic human override）下建立了纯策略团队最优策略的存在性与表征，并为线性二次系统推导了闭合形式的递推式，实现了与连续状态无关的高效计算。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的共享自主权威分配与转移方法通常依赖于控制输入的混合（blending）或启发式切换规则，缺乏理论保证，且未能考虑权威转移的动态特性。因此需要一种有理论根基的、能显式建模权威切换动态的机制。

Method: 将控制切换问题建模为相同利益动态博弈（identical-interest dynamic game），权威转移嵌入系统动态中；在随机人类覆盖下证明纯策略团队最优政策的存与表征；对线性二次系统推导最优切换策略和价值函数的闭合形式递推，实现不依赖连续状态的高效计算。

Result: 在标量和多维线性系统上验证了框架，展示了最优切换如何适应变化的系统动力学、成本结构和覆盖概率，揭示了人类适应性与自主效率之间的基本权衡。

Conclusion: 将共享自主建立在合作博弈论基础上具有实际价值，可为权威分配与转移提供具有理论保证的最优策略，而非启发式规则。

Abstract: Shared autonomy requires principled mechanisms for allocating and transferring control between a human and an autonomous agent. Existing approaches often rely on blending control inputs or heuristic switching rules, which lack theoretical guarantees and fail to account for the dynamics of authority transfer. This paper develops a cooperative game-theoretic framework for authority switching in shared autonomy. We formulate the control switching problem as an identical-interest dynamic game in which authority transitions are embedded into the system dynamics, yielding optimal switching policies rather than ad hoc rules. We establish the existence and characterization of team-optimal policies in pure strategies under stochastic human override, accounting for asymmetric authority where humans retain override capability. For linear-quadratic systems, we derive closed-form recursions for the optimal switching policies and value functions, enabling efficient computation independent of the continuous state. We validate the framework on scalar and multi-dimensional linear systems, demonstrating how optimal switching adapts to varying system dynamics, cost structures, and override probabilities. The results reveal fundamental trade-offs between human adaptability and autonomous efficiency, illustrating the practical benefits of grounding shared autonomy in cooperative game theory.

### [2] [Valhalla: A Layered Knowledge-State and Service-Governance Framework for Long-Term Scientific Knowledge Work](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15193)
*Yuyang Zheng,Nan Li,Wenxia Deng,Lige Yan,Xiang Li,Si Chen*

Main category: q-bio.NC

TL;DR: 本文提出Valhalla，一个面向长期科学知识工作的分层知识状态与服务治理框架。该框架通过五层FREG模型（File-Resource-Entity-Relationship-Graph）取代扁平图结构，并引入Router-Contract-Workflow服务治理架构（受微内核范式启发），使不同研究者的知识状态能够在统一结构下交换与重组。实验在抗体设计审阅任务中验证了知识摄入、跨成员整合与科学写作支持。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有LLM智能体的结构化知识系统多为节点中心（node-centric）设计，依赖个人组织实践，限制了知识共享、整合和跨用户重组能力。

Method: 提出五层FREG模型（File-Resource-Entity-Relationship-Graph）：File和Resource保留源身份与溯源，Entity表示知识对象，Relationship捕获语义判断，Graph提供面向任务的知识视图。同时提出Router-Contract-Workflow服务治理架构，约束LLM对知识状态的访问、修改和扩展，保证结构一致性和可审计操作边界。

Result: 通过抗体设计审阅任务（26篇论文资源、80个知识实体、92个语义关系）验证了知识摄入、跨成员集成和科学写作支持的有效性。

Conclusion: Valhalla不是提出新的知识抽取算法，而是提供一种组织协作科学知识的范式，将个体化知识结构转化为可迁移、可重组的共享知识状态。

Abstract: As large language model (LLM) agents are increasingly adopted in scientific research, external knowledge bases, knowledge graphs, and long-term memory have improved information retrieval and task continuity. However, most structured knowledge systems remain node-centric, representing files, concepts, results, and judgments as nodes and relations in a graph. While suitable for personal knowledge management, such structures often depend on individual organizational practices, limiting knowledge sharing, integration, and reorganization across users. This paper presents Valhalla, a layered knowledge-state and service-governance framework for long-term scientific knowledge work. Valhalla replaces flat graphs with layered encapsulation and stable semantic boundaries through a five-layer File-Resource-Entity-Relationship-Graph (FREG) model. File and Resource preserve source identity and provenance, Entity represents knowledge objects, Relationship captures semantic judgments, and Graph provides task-oriented knowledge views, enabling knowledge states from different researchers to be exchanged and reorganized under a unified structure. We further introduce a Router-Contract-Workflow service-governance architecture, inspired by the microkernel paradigm, to constrain how language models access, modify, and extend knowledge states while maintaining structural consistency and auditable operational boundaries. We implement a Valhalla prototype and validate knowledge ingestion, cross-member integration, and scientific writing support through an antibody-design review task comprising 26 paper resources, 80 knowledge entities, and 92 semantic relations. Rather than proposing a new knowledge-extraction algorithm, Valhalla offers a paradigm for organizing collaborative scientific knowledge, transforming individualized knowledge structures into transferable and reorganizable shared knowledge states.

### [3] [Observational Manifestations of Primordial Objects in the Early Universe Through the Hydrogen Subordinate Lines](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15481)
*Viktor K. Dubrovich,Yury N. Eroshenko,Stanislav I. Shirokov*

Main category: astro-ph.CO

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种新的机制，解释宇宙微波背景辐射（CMB）在原始致密天体（如原初黑洞）附近的光谱-空间畸变。在红移z~1000至~100的氢重组后，这些天体辐射的少数eV光子会显著增加周围氢原子亚稳态（n=2,3,4及更高能级）的布居数，从而在CMB中形成类似夫琅禾费吸收的光谱特征。这种畸变源于该时期物质与残余辐射之间的温度差，最终表现为环绕天体的环形吸收或发射特征，这些氢谱线位于射电波段，可通过大型射电望远镜观测。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探索在重组后时代，利用CMB谱畸变作为探测原始致密天体（如原初黑洞）的新观测手段，填补该红移区间对这类天体观测的空白。

Method: 提出并分析一种新的物理机制：原初黑洞周围的吸积盘辐射少量eV级光子，激发氢原子至n=2,3,4及更高能级，增加激发态布居数；由于物质与辐射温度差，在CMB中形成夫琅禾费型吸收/发射谱线畸变，并对观测可行性和射电波段信号强度进行估算。

Result: 分析表明该效应产生的氢复合线畸变位于射电波段，其信号强度足以被计划中的大型射电望远镜所探测到，可观察到围绕天体的环形吸收或发射特征。

Conclusion: 该机制为探测原初黑洞等致密天体提供了新的观测窗口，具有在射电波段通过CMB畸变成像实现天体探测的潜力。

Abstract: A new mechanism for the formation of spectral--spatial distortions in cosmic microwave background radiation near primordial massive compact objects (such as primordial black holes) at redshifts from $z \sim 1000$ to $\sim$100 is proposed. After hydrogen recombination, the radiation from these objects leads to a significant increase in the population of hydrogen subordinate levels in their surrounding environment. Consequently, this allows for the observation of a Fraunhofer-like absorption spectrum in the cosmic microwave background. Such a distortion is formed due to the temperature difference between matter and relic radiation at the corresponding epoch. Ultimately, we should observe circular absorption or emission features around the objects with small angular sizes. These subordinate hydrogen lines currently lie in the radio wavelength range. Estimates indicate that the effect under consideration is accessible for the observations with planned large radiotelescopes. The possibility of the proposed mechanism lies in the ability of an object (e.g., accretion disk around black hole) to emit few-eV photons that populate the $n=2,3,4$ and higher levels of hydrogen, enabling the required excitation.
