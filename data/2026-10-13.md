Motivation: 数据驱动超声研究受限于大规模开放原始通道数据的稀缺。现有公开数据集多为重建图像，少数开放原始通道数据通常面向单一重建或成像任务，难以支撑通用方法与基础模型。且像误差校正、混响抑制等关键挑战必须依赖原始通道数据。

Method: 构建社区驱动的开放数据计划OpenH-RF，汇集学术界与工业界38家机构的数据，包含19,332次采集、约40 TB医学超声原始通道数据，涵盖在体、体模和仿真数据；为每个数据集标注所支持的任务；设计统一、自包含的数据格式，同时保存原始通道数据及采集、探头、元数据和重建信息，以确保跨扫描仪和采集方案的可复现处理。

Result: 形成约40 TB、19,332次采集的公开语料库，支持广义重建、声速估计、误差校正、血流成像、运动估计、定量超声和图像解读等任务；数据以CC BY 4.0许可公开托管，可程序化、选择性、规模化流式访问。

Conclusion: 通过将异构超声数据纳入统一表示并依托现代数据基础设施，OpenH-RF降低了使用原始通道数据的门槛，旨在向新社区开放医学超声研究。项目主页：https://open-h.github.io/OpenH-RF。

Abstract: Medical ultrasound is a widely accessible, real-time imaging modality, yet progress in data-driven ultrasound research is constrained by the scarcity of large, open collections of raw channel data. Existing public datasets are predominantly based on reconstructed images, while the smaller number of open raw channel datasets is generally specialized to individual reconstruction or imaging tasks, limiting the development of general-purpose methods and foundation models that operate directly on ultrasound signals. While interpretive tasks can be performed on reconstructed images, many of the key technical challenges in ultrasound imaging, such as aberration correction and reverberation reduction, require the raw channel data itself. We introduce OpenH-RF, a community-driven open dataset initiative comprising approximately 40 TB of medical ultrasound raw channel data in 19,332 acquisitions, contributed by 38 institutions across academia and industry, spanning in-vivo, phantom, and simulated data. Each dataset is annotated with a number of supported tasks, ranging from generalized reconstruction, speed-of-sound estimation, and aberration correction to blood flow imaging, motion estimation, quantitative ultrasound, and image interpretation. OpenH-RF provides a unified, self-contained data format that captures raw channel data together with the acquisition, probe, metadata, and reconstruction information required for reproducible processing across scanners and acquisition schemes. The corpus is publicly hosted under a permissive CC BY 4.0 license and can be streamed programmatically and selectively at scale. By bringing heterogeneous ultrasound data into a common representation, backed by modern data infrastructure, OpenH-RF lowers the barrier to working with raw channel data and aims to open medical ultrasound research to new communities. See https://open-h.github.io/OpenH-RF.
