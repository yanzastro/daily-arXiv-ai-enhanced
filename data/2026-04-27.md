### [42] [Backlighting the Cosmic Web with Fast Radio Bursts: An Anthology of Dispersion Measure Cross-Correlations with Large-Scale Structure and Baryon Tracers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22105)
*Kritti Sharma,Elisabeth Krause,Vikram Ravi,Dhayaa Anbajagane,Liam Connor,W. L. Kimmy Wu,Simone Ferraro,Sebastian Grandis,David Alonso,Yi-Kuan Chiang,Casey J. Law,Pranjal R. S.,Samuel McCarty,Shivam Pandey*

Main category: astro-ph.CO

TL;DR: 本文利用CHIME/FRB观测的3,455个独特FRB源，通过其色散量与宇宙大尺度结构的十种示踪物进行相关性分析，测量到2.6-5σ的显著信号，表明FRB视线穿过高密环境时色散量系统性地增大。通过热气体示踪物的相关性约束反馈强度，排除了弱反馈模型（~3.5σ），为下一代FRB设施绘制宇宙网重子分布奠定了基础。

Motivation: FRB的色散量能够探测宇宙网中的重子分布，但此前缺乏大规模统计样本的系统性相关性研究。作者希望利用大规模FRB样本与多种大尺度结构示踪物进行交叉相关分析，以定量刻画FRB色散量与环境密度的关系，并约束星系反馈模型。

Method: 使用CHIME/FRB的3,455个独特FRB源（约15角分定位精度），测量其色散量与十种大尺度结构示踪物的相关性，包括星系分布、弱引力透镜、宇宙红外背景、CMB透镜、热Sunyaev-Zel'dovich效应、X射线辐射（星系团和超团）、软X射线背景以及射电连续谱辐射。通过DM-z关系推断的重子分布模型预测与观测的相关性强度进行比较，约束反馈强度。

Result: 获得了十种示踪物与FRB色散量的显著相关信号（2.6-5σ），表明FRB视线穿过高密环境时色散量系统性地增大。热SZ×DM和软X射线背景×DM的相关振幅与中等反馈模型一致（~0.5σ），弱反馈模型被软X射线背景×DM相关性排除（~3.5σ）。

Conclusion: 这些多示踪物测量为FRB宇宙学建立了定量基础，预示着下一代FRB设施（BURSTT、CHORD、DSA、SKA）将能与其他探针协同，绘制宇宙网完整区域的重子含量分布图。

Abstract: Fast Radio Bursts (FRBs) probe baryons permeating the cosmic web through their dispersion measures (DMs), which encode the integrated electron density along cosmological sightlines. Using 3,455 unique FRB sources from CHIME/FRB with $\sim 15$ arcmin localizations, we present an anthology of DM correlations with tracers of large-scale structure and baryonic matter at redshifts $z \lesssim 1.5$. We measure statistically significant correlations at $2.6-5σ$ with ten probes, including galaxies ($2.8σ$), weak gravitational lensing ($2.6σ$), cosmic infrared background ($4.0σ$), cosmic microwave background (CMB) lensing ($3.3σ$), thermal Sunyaev Zel'dovich (tSZ) effect ($3.8σ$), X-ray emission tracing galaxy clusters ($5.0σ$) and superclusters ($3.3σ$), soft X-ray background (SXRB, $4.1σ$), and radio continuum emission ($3.2σ$). These measurements reveal a consistent picture in which FRB sightlines intersecting overdense environments carry systematically larger DMs. Correlations with hot-gas tracers provide additional leverage on the strength of feedback, as they are strongly weighted towards the dense, bound gas. The measured amplitude of tSZ$\times$DM and SXRB$\times$DM correlations are consistent with theoretical predictions of baryon distribution from a DM-$z$ relation-inferred model with moderate feedback at $\sim 0.5σ$ level. Weaker feedback scenario is ruled out at $\sim 3.5σ$ by the SXRB$\times$DM correlation. Taken together, these measurements constitute a quantitative multi-tracer foundation for a new era in which FRBs from next generation facilities, such as BURSTT, CHORD, DSA, and SKA, in harmony with other probes, will map the baryon content of the full extent of the cosmic web.

### [43] [Constraints on the Primordial Black Hole Abundance using Pulsar Parameter Drifts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22634)
*Yan-Chen Bi,Yu-Mei Wu,Qing-Guo Huang*

Main category: astro-ph.CO

TL;DR: 该论文首次利用脉冲星参数漂移搜索原初黑洞产生的标量诱导引力波，在95%置信水平上给出原初黑洞丰度上限$f_{\mathrm{PBH}} < 10^{-10}$（质量范围$[3 \times 10^{-1}, 4 \times 10^{4}] M_{\odot}$），强烈排除了当前LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA探测到的双黑洞系统起源于原初黑洞的可能性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 原初黑洞为解释地面引力波探测器观测到的双黑洞（尤其是理论质量间隙内的双黑洞）提供了引人注目的解释，但需要检验其可行性。

Method: 利用早期宇宙中产生原初黑洞的标量扰动会不可避免地产生标量诱导引力波（SIGWs），这些引力波在亚毫赫兹频段表现为脉冲星自转周期的二阶导数（自转加加速度）中的类急动度漂移，通过搜索这种漂移来限制原初黑洞丰度。

Result: 在95%置信水平上给出原初黑洞丰度上限$f_{\mathrm{PBH}} < 10^{-10}$，覆盖质量范围$[3 \times 10^{-1}, 4 \times 10^{4}] M_{\odot}$。

Conclusion: 该结果强烈不支持当前LVK合作组探测到的双黑洞起源于原初黑洞的假说。

Abstract: Primordial black holes (PBHs) provide a compelling interpretation for the binary black holes (BBHs) observed by ground-based gravitational-wave (GW) detectors, especially for those BBHs in the theoretical mass gap. In the early Universe, the scalar perturbations required to produce such PBHs inevitably generate scalar-induced GWs (SIGWs). These SIGWs peak in the sub-nanohertz band, and manifest secularly as measurable jerk-like drifts in the second derivative of pulsar spin periods. In this Letter, we perform the first search for SIGWs using pulsar parameter drifts, and place a 95\% confidence-level upper limit on the PBH abundance of $f_{\mathrm{PBH}} < 10^{-10}$ over the mass range $[3 \times 10^{-1}, 4 \times 10^{4}] M_{\odot}$. Our results strongly disfavor a PBH origin for the BBHs currently detected by the LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA (LVK) Collaborations.

### [44] [Precision Analysis for $\boldsymbol{H_0}$ Using Upcoming Multi-band Gravitational Wave Observations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22731)
*Setabuddin,Md Riajul Haque,Ratna Koley,Supratik Pal*

Main category: astro-ph.CO

TL;DR: 本文研究了利用原初黑洞（PBH）产生的多波段引力波（GW）观测来约束哈勃参数（$H_0$）不确定性。结合标量诱导和并合诱导的引力波信号，使用SKA和ET探测器进行多波段分析。通过信噪比估计和Fisher矩阵分析，发现当PBH参数精度达到1%时，$H_0$不确定性可降至$\mathcal{O}(0.1)~{\rm km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}$，提供了一种独立于宇宙距离阶梯的哈勃参数测量方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 当前宇宙学中哈勃常数（$H_0$）存在显著争议（哈勃张力），需要独立的、不依赖于传统宇宙距离阶梯的测量方法。多波段引力波观测提供了一种潜在的新途径，而原初黑洞作为可能的引力波源，为这种测量提供了新的物理载体。

Method: 将原初黑洞产生的标量诱导引力波和并合诱导引力波相结合，利用SKA（射电波段）和ET（地面探测器）实现多波段联合分析。采用信噪比（SNR）估计和Fisher矩阵分析，对PBH参数（质量$M_{\rm PBH}$和丰度$f_{\rm PBH}$）进行统计预测，并传播这些不确定性以估计$H_0$的约束精度。

Result: 在保守情形（参数精度$\leq 10\%$）下，$H_0$不确定性$\lesssim 2~{\rm km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}$；在乐观情形（参数精度$\leq 1\%$）下，$H_0$不确定性可降至$\lesssim \mathcal{O}(0.1)~{\rm km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}$。结果对$H_0$的基准选择不敏感，仅对PBH坍缩效率有中等程度依赖。

Conclusion: 多波段引力波观测能够为哈勃参数的不确定性约束提供独立且互补的方法，有潜力成为一种不依赖传统宇宙距离阶梯的哈勃参数测量新手段。

Abstract: We investigate how multi-band gravitational wave (GW) observations can constrain the uncertainties in the Hubble parameter ($H_0$) using primordial black holes (PBHs) as possible sources. Our framework combines scalar-induced and merger-induced GWs from PBHs, and forecasts on a combination of two future detectors Square Kilometre Array (SKA) and the Einstein Telescope (ET), enabling a multi-band analysis. We perform a statistical forecast of the PBH parameters, $M_{\rm PBH}$ and $f_{\rm PBH}$, using signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) estimates and Fisher matrix analysis. Imposing $\mathrm{SNR} \geq 1$, we identify the accessible PBH parameter space and propagate these uncertainties to estimate the corresponding uncertainties in $H_0$. For $δθ_i/θ_i \leq 0.1$, with $θ_i \equiv M_{\rm PBH}(f_{\rm PBH})$, we find $δH_0 \lesssim 2~{\rm km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}$ in a conservative approach, improving to $δH_0 \lesssim \mathcal{O}(0.1)~{\rm km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}$ for $δθ_i/θ_i \leq 0.01$ for an optimistic approach of precision measurement. The results are further found to be largely insensitive to the fiducial choice of the $H_0$, with only moderate dependence on the PBH collapse efficiency. These findings demonstrate that multi-band GW observations provide an independent and complementary approach to constraining the uncertainties in $H_0$, with the potential to provide a novel, cosmic distance ladder-independent measure of the Hubble parameter.

### [45] [On the redshift evolution of the spin parameter in cosmological simulations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22738)
*Tomas Riera,Alexander Knebe,Chris Power,Robert Adriel Mostoghiu Paun,Adam Ussing*

Main category: astro-ph.CO

TL;DR: 本文利用LCDM N-body模拟，系统研究了暗物质晕自旋参数（Peebles λ和Bullock λ'）在红移0<z<5范围内的演化规律，发现ln(λ)呈轻微线性演化，ln(λ')在z~1-2处存在非单调转折，并提供了闭合形式的拟合函数，作为红移依赖的自旋分布替代传统恒定分布假设。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 暗物质晕自旋参数的对数正态分布已在固定红移下被广泛认知，但其均值和弥散的定量红移演化尚未被充分探索，先前研究要么缺乏足够的质量分辨率以建立可靠的演化趋势，要么未提供可用于正向建模的解析关系。

Method: 使用一套在红移范围0<z<5内具有受控分辨率的LCDM N-body模拟套件，分别表征Peebles (λ)和Bullock (λ')两种自旋定义的均值和弥散的演化，并验证结果不受质量分辨率或晕定义选择的影响。

Result: 发现ln(λ)具有轻微但统计上稳健的线性演化趋势；ln(λ')呈现非单调演化，在z~1-2处存在转折；两种趋势均不受质量分辨率或晕定义选择的影响。

Conclusion: 提供了闭合形式的拟合函数，使建模者能在有效红移范围内获取物理上合理的自旋值，为半经验模型和半解析星系形成模型提供了实用的红移依赖替代方案，取代了常见的恒定自旋分布假设。

Abstract: Although the spin parameter of dark matter halos is well known to follow a log-normal distribution at fixed epoch, its quantitative redshift evolution - encompassing both the mean and the dispersion - remains only partially explored. Prior studies either lack the mass resolution required to establish reliable evolutionary trends or do not provide analytical relations that enable forward modelling. Using a suite of LCDM N-body simulations with controlled resolution across the redshift range 0 < z < 5, we characterise the evolution of the mean and dispersion of the Peebles (lambda) and Bullock (lambda') definitions of spin. We find a mild but statistically robust linear evolution for ln(lambda) and a non-monotonic trend with a turnover at z ~ 1 - 2 for ln lambda', which we verify are unaffected by mass resolution of choice of halo definition. We provide closed-form fitting functions for these trends that allow modellers to draw physically motivated spin values at any redshift within our range of validity. This is a practical, redshift-dependent alternative to the common assumption of a constant spin distribution, and provides a useful input to semi-empirical and semi-analytic models of galaxy formation.
