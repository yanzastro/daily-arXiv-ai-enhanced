Motivation: 宇宙钟可用于测量H(z)和H₀，但现有文献中高纯度样本数量有限，候选体纯度会显著影响统计不确定度，因此需要扩充高纯度候选体池以提高宇宙学参数测量的稳健性。

Method: 在0.03<z<0.4范围内，利用GAMA测光的NUVrJ颜色选择静止星系，结合光谱特征（Hα、[OII]、Hδ）作为恒星形成活动示踪，并计算Ca II H/K线比值判断星族年龄；仅保留大质量星系并辅以光谱和图像的目视检查以确认候选体。

Result: 筛选出231个大质量被动演化椭圆星系作为宇宙钟候选体。与母样本相比，候选体比恒星形成率(sSFR)显著更低，中位数约为10⁻¹² Myr⁻¹；中位年龄比母样本老约2 Gyr。

Conclusion: 该工作扩充了高纯度宇宙钟候选体样本，并量化了纯度对统计不确定度的影响，为未来更稳健的H(z)和H₀计算提供了基础。

Abstract: We present a pure sample of 231 massive, passively evolving elliptical galaxies as cosmic chronometer candidates selected from the Galaxy and Mass Assembly (GAMA) survey between $0.03<z<0.4$. The goal of this work is to increase the pool of high-purity cosmic chronometer candidates available in the literature and assess the effect of purity on statistical uncertainty to make $H(z)$ and $H_0$ calculations more robust in future studies. We use traditional selection criteria to identify cosmic chronometers using photometry and spectroscopy in conjunction with modelled parameters such as stellar mass and velocity dispersion. We use an NUVrJ photometric selection to identify quiescent galaxies and identify a number of spectral features, including H$α$, [OII] and H$δ$ to use as tracers for ongoing or recently halted star formation. Additionally, as part of the selection, we calculate the ratio between the Ca II H and K lines to determine whether a galaxy's stellar population is dominated by older or younger stars. We confirm CC candidacy by selecting only the most massive galaxies and perform a visual inspection of the spectra and images of CC candidates. We provide an analysis of the parent sample in comparison to the CC sample and confirm CCs have much lower sSFR, with a median sSFR of $10^{-12}~\text{Myr}^{-1}$. Additionally, we find their median ages are $\sim2~\text{Gyr}$ older than the parent sample.
