Although these metrics detect a transition, they do not reveal how the new state relates to the repertoire represented by the neural manifold. Here, I introduce a geometric decomposition that separates changes attributable to gain modulation of a nearby manifold state from movement within the manifold and genuine off-manifold displacement. The approach uses the radial axis of neural population activity to partition the normal space of a local manifold region.
